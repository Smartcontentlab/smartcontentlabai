The problem

Bad websites quietly tax small businesses every day.

A local customer lands on the site, gets confused, doubts the business, fails to find proof, and leaves. The owner sees “low traffic” when the real issue is often trust, clarity, speed, and friction.

Our audit is designed to expose the exact leaks: unclear messaging, weak CTAs, thin service pages, poor mobile layout, missing reviews, slow media, no lead magnet, and no follow-up system.

01

Unclear first impression

Visitors need to understand who you help, what you do, where you work, and why to trust you within seconds.

02

Missing conversion path

Most sites have a contact page but no persuasive path from pain point to proof to action.

03

Weak trust signals

Reviews, guarantees, before-and-after work, credentials, service areas, and fast response promises reduce buyer anxiety.

04

No follow-up engine

A form submission should trigger qualification, reminders, estimates, review requests, and nurture — not disappear into an inbox.
