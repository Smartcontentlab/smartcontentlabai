The Genteel Story

Born in the Outer Sunset. Raised on hard work.

Genteel Plumbers was founded in 2016 by Graham W. Jones in San Francisco's Outer Sunset district. We understand how devastating a plumbing emergency can be — which is why same-day service has been our standard from day one.

Our trucks are rolling workshops, stocked with the newest technology and tools. And our plumbers aren't passing through — they're career craftspeople with a genuine passion for the trade. Customer service isn't a department here. It's practically in our DNA.
