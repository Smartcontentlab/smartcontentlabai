02 / About the House

A curator’s eye.
A neighbor’s handshake.

No.17 started as a single rolling rack at the Ridgewood flea — one obsessive collector, forty flawless pieces, and a rule that never changed: if it isn’t extraordinary, it doesn’t make the rack. Seven years on, the archive fills a former tailor’s shop off Woodward Avenue, but every piece is still sourced the slow way — estates, ateliers, and closets with stories.

Our promise: every garment is inspected stitch by stitch, dated against house archives, and leaves with a signed certificate of authenticity. Wrong-era hardware, replaced linings, “too good to be true” — we catch it so you never have to. If it isn’t right, you get every dollar back.

— The Founder & Curator, No.17
